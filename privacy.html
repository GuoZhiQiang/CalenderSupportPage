Privacy Policy

隐私政策

Last updated: July 8, 2026

最后更新：2026年7月8日

This Privacy Policy describes how CalendarNotes ("the App") handles your information. Your privacy matters to us, and we've designed the App to keep your data on your own devices.

本隐私政策说明 CalendarNotes（"本应用"）如何处理你的信息。我们重视你的隐私，并将应用设计为把你的数据保存在你自己的设备上。

Data We Collect

我们收集的数据

We do not collect any personal data. The App does not have its own servers, does not use analytics or advertising SDKs, and the developer cannot access any of your information.

我们不收集任何个人数据。本应用没有自己的服务器，不使用任何分析或广告 SDK，开发者无法访问你的任何信息。

How Your Data Is Stored

你的数据如何存储

  • On your device: All reminders and settings you create are stored locally on your device.
  • 本机存储：你创建的所有提醒和设置都保存在你的设备本地。
  • iCloud (optional): If you enable iCloud Sync, your reminders are stored in your personal iCloud account and synced across your Apple devices. This data is managed by Apple under Apple's privacy terms — we never see it.
  • iCloud（可选）：若你开启 iCloud 同步，你的提醒会存储在你个人的 iCloud 账户中，并在你的 Apple 设备间同步。该数据由 Apple 根据其隐私条款管理——我们无法查看。

Notifications

通知

The App uses local notifications to remind you of your events. These are scheduled entirely on your device and are not sent through any external server.

本应用使用本地通知来提醒你的事件。这些通知完全在你的设备上安排，不经过任何外部服务器。

Purchases

购买

iCloud Sync is unlocked via a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple. All payment information is handled by Apple; we do not receive or store your payment details.

iCloud 同步通过 Apple 处理的一次性内购解锁。所有支付信息均由 Apple 处理；我们不会收到或存储你的支付详情。

Third Parties

第三方

The App does not share data with any third parties. The only Apple services involved (iCloud, In-App Purchase) are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

本应用不会与任何第三方共享数据。所涉及的仅有的 Apple 服务（iCloud、内购）受 Apple 隐私政策 约束。

Children's Privacy

儿童隐私

The App does not knowingly collect any information from children. Since we collect no data at all, the App is safe for users of all ages.

本应用不会有意收集任何儿童信息。由于我们完全不收集数据，本应用对所有年龄段用户都是安全的。

Changes to This Policy

本政策的变更

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

我们可能会不时更新本隐私政策。任何变更都会在此页面发布，并更新日期。
