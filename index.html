About

关于

CalendarNotes is a simple, focused reminder app. Create date-based alarms with descriptions and weekly repeats, view them in a list or calendar, and get local notifications when events are due. Optionally sync across your Apple devices via iCloud.

CalendarNotes 是一款简洁专注的提醒应用。你可以创建带描述和每周重复的日期闹钟，在列表或日历中查看，并在事件到期时收到本地通知。还可选择通过 iCloud 在你的 Apple 设备间同步。

Frequently Asked Questions

常见问题

I'm not receiving notifications
收不到通知怎么办？
Go to Settings → Notifications → CalendarNotes and make sure notifications are allowed. Also check that Focus / Do Not Disturb is not blocking them.
请前往 系统设置 → 通知 → CalendarNotes，确认已允许通知。同时检查专注模式 / 勿扰模式是否屏蔽了通知。
How does iCloud Sync work?
iCloud 同步如何使用？
After a one-time purchase, your reminders sync automatically across all devices signed in to the same iCloud account. Make sure iCloud Drive is enabled in your device settings.
一次性购买后，你的提醒会在所有登录同一 iCloud 账户的设备间自动同步。请确保设备设置中已开启 iCloud Drive。
How do I restore my purchase?
如何恢复购买？
Open Settings inside the app and tap "Restore Purchase". Your iCloud Sync unlock will be restored on any device signed in with your Apple ID.
在 App 内打开「设置」，点击「恢复购买」。你的 iCloud 同步解锁会在任何登录你 Apple ID 的设备上恢复。
Will my data be lost if I reinstall?
重装后数据会丢失吗？
If iCloud Sync is enabled, your data is restored automatically after reinstalling. Without it, data is stored only on the device.
若已开启 iCloud 同步，重装后数据会自动恢复。未开启时，数据仅保存在本机。
